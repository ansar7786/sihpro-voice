The Problem

Problem statement SIH26097 Ministry Social Justice and Empowerment Scheme PM-AJAY, GIA component Bucket Agriculture, FoodTech and Rural Development

AI-Driven Voice Assistant for Livelihood Mapping and NSQF-Aligned Skilling Recommendations for SC Communities under the GIA component of PM-AJAY.

Many beneficiaries cannot use text-heavy portals or course catalogues. Generic counselling ignores personal constraints such as distance, time, family duties and the wish for self-employment, so a recommendation that looks good on paper (too far, too long, wrong education level) never turns into an enrolment.

Implementing agencies also have no structured picture of what beneficiaries want, which skills are missing, and where training seats fall short of demand.

  • Voice conversations in local languages and dialects, not forms
  • Profiling of education, skills, assets, mobility and work preference
  • NSQF-aligned training and regional opportunity matching
  • Reach through IVR, WhatsApp voice notes and a lightweight web or kiosk interface

Our Solution

A livelihood decision-support system with a voice entry point. The language model understands the person. Rules and curated data decide what is actually feasible. The system never invents jobs, courses or statistics.

1. Voice to profile

The person talks to a live voice assistant that builds a structured profile. Unknown fields stay marked missing, and the profile is read back for the person to confirm or correct.

2. Pathway engine

Hard filters (education, duration, mobility, route, equipment) remove what is not feasible. Six factors rank the rest into two or three pathways.

3. Official view

Consented, anonymised profiles become demand by trade, common skill gaps and a gap between interested people and training seats.

Say "I cannot spend five months" or "I cannot travel" and the pathways recompute, showing which ones dropped out and why. If no verified opportunity exists, the system says so and still offers a training pathway.

Key Features

Live two-way voice

A spoken conversation in Telugu, Hindi and English through the Gemini Live API, with interruption handling and session resumption.

Missing, not guessed

The voice model only collects the profile. Every field is tagged stated, inferred or missing, and the person confirms before anything is recommended.

Constraint-aware pathways

Two or three feasible pathways with qualification, duration, skill gap and the nearest training centre.

Live what-if recalculation

Change maximum training months, mobility, self versus wage work or time available. The engine reruns in under a second and highlights what changed.

Explainable ranking

A "Why this pathway" panel shows High, Medium, Low or Unknown for interest, skill, education, duration, local demand and assets. No invented percentages.

Honest fallback

When no verified opportunity exists, the pathway says "No verified opportunity found" and still gives a training route.

Official view for agencies

Demand by trade, common skill gaps, and interested profiles against training seats per block. Small groups are hidden to protect privacy.

Privacy by design

Consent before recording, no caste, community or religion field, no exact address, and raw audio is not stored.

Assisted mode and accessibility

Large buttons, a font-size control and spoken-style prompts, so one facilitator can register many people.
